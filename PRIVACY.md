## English

**What the app does.** Nadlan IL answers questions about Israeli property sale prices using public deal records published by the Israel Tax Authority via govmap.gov.il.

**What we receive.** When the AI assistant calls the app, it sends only the tool inputs: an address or place name, and optional filters such as rooms, size, radius or time range. We do not receive your name, email, chat history or account details.

**How it is used.** The address is forwarded to the public Govmap service to look up coordinates and recorded deals. Results are returned to the assistant. Responses may be cached for up to 6 hours to reduce load.

**Storage.** We do not create user accounts, sell data, or use it for advertising. Standard server logs (time, request path, IP address) may be kept by our hosting provider for up to 30 days for security and debugging.

**Third parties.** Govmap (Government of Israel) receives the address you asked about. Our hosting provider ([HOSTING PROVIDER]) processes requests.

**Accuracy.** Data comes from official records and may be incomplete or delayed. Estimates are statistical and are not a professional appraisal.
