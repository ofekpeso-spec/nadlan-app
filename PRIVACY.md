## English

**What the app does.** Nadlan IL answers questions about Israeli neighborhoods (urban renewal, construction sites, subsidized housing lotteries, housing supply) using official open data from data.gov.il.

**What we receive.** When the AI assistant calls the app, it sends only the tool inputs: a city and optional street name, or a dataset search term. We do not receive your name, email, chat history or account details.

**How it is used.** The city/street text is sent to the public data.gov.il API to find matching records. Results are returned to the assistant. Responses may be cached for up to 12 hours to reduce load.

**Storage.** We do not create user accounts, sell data, or use it for advertising. Standard server logs (time, request path, IP address) may be kept by our hosting provider for up to 30 days for security and debugging.

**Third parties.** data.gov.il (Government of Israel) receives the city/street text you asked about. Our hosting provider ([HOSTING PROVIDER]) processes requests.

**Accuracy.** Data comes from official records and may be incomplete or delayed. It is general information, not legal, planning or financial advice.
